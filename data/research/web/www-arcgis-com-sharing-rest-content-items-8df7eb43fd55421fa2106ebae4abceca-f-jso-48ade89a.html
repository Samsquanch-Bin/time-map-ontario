The Ontario Provincial Plaque Program is the Trust's oldest and perhaps best-known activity, with nearly 1,300 familiar blue and gold plaques in communities across Ontario. The plaques enliven Ontario's history, sharing stories of the people, places, and events that helped shape the province. They enrich our sense of place and introduce newcomers and visitors to the unique character of regions, communities, and rations within the province.<\/span>
