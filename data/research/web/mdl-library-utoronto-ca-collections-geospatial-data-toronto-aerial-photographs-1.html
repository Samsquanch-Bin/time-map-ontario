Toronto Aerial Photographs 1947 (black and white and colourized)

These air photos were georeferenced from originals held at the City of Toronto archives. The text files included in the downloads are the ground control points used for all original images. The colourized photo versions are derived from the georeferenced images using deep learning in ArcGIS Pro. To view a mosaiced view of the photos, click on the following link https://maps.library.utoronto.ca/tiles1947/index.html
