Title

Emerson Bristol Biggar Collection

Description

Eight volumes that Emerson Bristol Biggar donated to the Hamilton Public Library in 1904. Titles (from left to right): L'Acadie, or Seven Years' Exploration in British America Vol. 1 and Vol. 2 by James Edward Alexander; The Coming of the Princess, and Other Poems by Kate Seymour MacLean; Twilight of Faith by Elizabeth Frame; The Aeolian Harp by Sarah Herbert; Views of Canada and the Colonists by James B. Brown; The U. E. by William Kirby; and A Little Book of Canadian Essays by Lawrence J. Burpee.

Source

Local History and Archives Hamilton Public Library
