This skirmish took place at the village of Fort Erie, then called Waterloo, on 2 June 1866 during the Fenian raids, the same day as the larger Battle of Ridgeway. Canadian volunteers from the Welland Field Battery and the Dunnville Naval Brigade, under Lieutenant-Colonel John Dennis, travelled aboard the tugboat W.T. Robb to secure the village and cut off the invaders' escape route. Many of the gunners carried outdated weapons. They landed and captured around 59 Fenians, but John O'Neill's main force then returned from Ridgeway. In the firefight that followed the outnumbered Canadians were mostly forced to surrender, while those on the boat returned to Port Colborne. Large Canadian reinforcements later surrounded the Fenians, and O'Neill retreated toward New York State. Some Fenians deserted across the river, and about 850 surrendered to a United States naval party from USS Michigan near Buffalo, ending the Fenian incursions on the Niagara Peninsula.
