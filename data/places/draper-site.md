The Draper Site is an ancestral Wendat (Huron) village on a stream feeding West Duffins Creek in north Pickering, about 35 kilometres north-east of Toronto. The article calls it late fifteenth-century, yet also says the community grew over roughly thirty years from around 1525; later radiocarbon work on the related Mantle Site has also led to the Draper Site being re-dated, so its exact years are uncertain. The village was enlarged at least five times as smaller nearby villages were abandoned. At its peak it held 35 longhouses and up to 2,000 people on about four hectares, inside several rows of wooden palisades. After more than a generation, the whole community moved about five kilometres north-west to found the village now called the Mantle Site. Archaeologists fully excavated the largely undisturbed site in 1975 and 1978 ahead of planned construction of the Pickering Airport. Today it lies in woodland on farmland, reachable on foot from the end of North Road.
