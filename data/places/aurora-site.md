The Aurora Site, also called the "Old Fort" or "Hill Fort", is an ancestral Wendat village near Vandorf in Whitchurch–Stouffville, on a headwater stream of the East Holland River north of the Oak Ridges Moraine. The village covered about 3.4 hectares and was protected by several rows of palisades. The source describes it as sixteenth-century but also says the community arrived around 1625, probably moving together from the Mantle Site nine kilometres to the south-east; the dating of these related sites is debated. It was lived in at the same time as the nearby Ratcliff Site, and the Rouge River trail between Lake Ontario and Lake Simcoe ran through it. Collectors looted the site heavily in the eighteenth and early nineteenth centuries and much of what they took is lost. The University of Toronto excavated it completely in 1947 and 1957, its first student dig, and that work helped show that the Wendat came together as a people in this region.
