Westfield Heritage Village is a heritage centre just west of Rockton, with more than 30 historic buildings on an 840-acre site run by the Hamilton Conservation Authority. It was started in 1961, when two Brantford high school teachers, Glenn Kilmer and Golden Macdonnell, used their own money to buy land there. Their aim was to rescue heritage buildings at risk of demolition and to teach people about pioneer life. Glenn's wife Doreen Kilmer helped develop and run the site. The donated buildings were rebuilt on site, and the Westfield Pioneer Village opened unofficially in 1963 and officially in 1964. Wentworth County bought it in 1968. It closed during a financial crisis in 1984 and was later redeveloped and renamed the Westfield Heritage Centre. Little is recorded in the source about the original histories of the buildings themselves.
