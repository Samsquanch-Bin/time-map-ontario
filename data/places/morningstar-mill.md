Morningstar Mill is a heritage site at Decew Falls on the Niagara Escarpment in St. Catharines. After the War of 1812, landowner John DeCou built a stone grist mill with an overhead waterwheel at the falls. In 1872 Robert Chappel built a new stone grist mill, called Mountain Mills, on the old mill's foundations, powered by a turbine. The St. Catharines Waterworks bought it in 1875, and once its water supply was restored it was sold to Wilson Morningstar. He was paid one-twelfth of the flour or feed the mill produced, and he also ran blacksmith and carpentry shops on the property. After a fire in 1895 left only the stone structure standing, he rebuilt. Morningstar died in 1933, and his family carried on the mill. The city bought the property in 1983, and volunteers restored the grist mill to working order in 1992.
